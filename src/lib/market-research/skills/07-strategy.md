# Stage 7 — Content Strategy & Article Planning

## Goal
Build an editorial cluster strategy of supporting informational articles linked directly to the target commercial collections to establish topical authority.

## Rules
- Target informational intent keywords with strong buyer alignment.
- Map explicit internal link blueprints (inbound and outbound) between articles and collection pages.
- Assign editorial priorities and content outlines for high-impact search volume capture.

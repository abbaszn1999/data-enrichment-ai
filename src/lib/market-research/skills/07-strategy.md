# Stage 7 — Content Strategy & Article Planning

## Goal
Turn the informational keywords that survived intent classification into a publishing plan: one article per keyword, each with the title an editor would ship, the right article format, and outbound links to the collection pages that can fulfil the intent.

## Rules
- One article per keyword. Never merge two keywords into one row and never split one keyword across rows.
- The title must preserve the keyword's search intent — the reader has to recognise their own question in it.
- Volume and difficulty are copied verbatim from the source keyword. Never re-estimate them.
- Outbound links point only at collection pages supplied to you, referenced the way the input asks. Never write, guess, or complete a URL, slug, or handle.
- Articles link out to collections; collections do not link back from this stage.
